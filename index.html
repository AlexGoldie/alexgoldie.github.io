Alexander D. Goldie

PhD Student, University of Oxford

I am a PhD student at the University of Oxford, supervised by Jakob Foerster and Shimon Whiteson. My research focuses on (meta-)reinforcement learning, automated algorithm discovery, and autonomous research and discovery.

I'm currently a research intern at Meta on the AI Research Agents team, supervised by Yoram Bachrach. Before that, I was a Research Scientist Intern at Wayve, working on offline reinforcement learning for autonomous driving. My PhD is offered by AIMS, a competitive PhD-level course for Machine Learning.
